## 0. Mission and invariants

Turn independently operated AI research and engineering into **reproducible, honest, permissioned, useful public learning**. The objective is to help a global collective question, reproduce, improve, and reuse findings—not convert generated claims into marketing certainty.

Publication is a **separate protected convergence domain**. Research code acceptance does not authorize publication; a reproducible experiment does not automatically make its interpretation reliable; a valid source link is not a license to reuse copyrighted text or imagery; a viral post is not evidence of understanding or societal benefit.

**Invariant 1:** A public factual claim binds to *specific* evidence, source revisions and a verified confidence/limitation statement.

**Invariant 2:** A fictional simulation may be published only with unambiguous simulation status and no invented results passed off as real.

**Invariant 3:** Experimenter, independent scientific/methodological reviewer, editorial reviewer and publishing principal are separately authorized functions. Different AI model names are not automatically independent control identities.

**Invariant 4:** Robots may prepare, edit, translate, storyboard, create accessible derivatives, and schedule *proposals* under scoped grants. The public publish operation requires effective separate permission and all applicable review gates. Future low-risk auto-publishing would need a separately approved scoped policy.

**Invariant 5:** Corrections, withdrawals, or stronger contradictory evidence must trigger a re-evaluation of *all known active derivatives*, including future scheduled posts, not just update a GitHub README.

**Invariant 6:** Human/collective contributors own their work and consent boundaries. Public provenance and auditability do not override privacy, licenses or deletion duties.

## 1. Canonical entities

| Object | Key and version | Essential properties |
|---|---|---|
| Study | `study_id` + `study_revision` | Question, background, stakeholders, author/collective, scope |
| Preregistered protocol | `protocol_id` + digest | Hypothesis, primary outcomes, control, treatment, metrics, exclusions, failure criteria, analysis plan |
| Run | `run_id` | Real execution only, timestamp, exact task corpus/model/tool/environment versions, cost, run logs, failed attempts, policy |
| Artifact | `artifact_id` + digest | Code/data/report, type, rights, privacy level, source provenance |
| Claim | `claim_id` + `claim_revision` | Atomic conclusion, certainty class, applicability, exclusions, evidence IDs |
| Independent review | `review_id` | Reviewer identity and conflicts, method, verdict, reproducibility scope and limitations |
| Publication decision | `decision_id` | Authorized content owner, approval scope, versions, target channels, expiry |
| Content derivative | `content_id` + digest | Script/video/article, language, claim IDs, visual rights, disclosures, accessibility, destination |
| Distribution event | `distribution_id` | Published URL/time, publisher, exact approved content/version |
| Correction event | `correction_id` | Trigger, affected claims, severity, remediation, notified derivatives, completion evidence |
| Collective contribution | `contribution_id` | Attribution, consent, rights, local-context review, feedback status |

Use W3C PROV-style links (entity generated by activity, used source, responsible agent) when suitable. Authentication and correctness require additional systems; merely using PROV vocabulary does not certify truth.

## 2. Publication state axes (not one magic status)

### Research
`question_proposed → protocol_drafted → protocol_reviewed → execution_authorized → executed → results_reviewed`

`execution_authorized` must never imply `executed`. A simulation has a separate `simulation` kind and may never create a real run-result record.

### Claims
`hypothesis | simulation | observed_single_source | observed_executed | independently_checked | independently_reproduced | disputed | corrected | withdrawn`

These are distinct **evidence classifications**, not a linear ladder. In particular, independent checking of provenance and independent reproduction of outcomes are different acts. Never badge with ACM marks or equivalent unless the actual external program awarded them.

### Publishing
`draft → fact_review → rights_privacy_review → approved → published | blocked | revoked`

### Maintenance
`current | review_due | materially_changed | correction_required | corrected | retracted`

A specific content derivative must reference allowed claim revisions, approved narrative caveats and actual source. A research paper may be published while its claims remain disputed, provided disagreements are accurately explained and approval criteria are met.

## 3. Publication gate decision table

| Proposed action | Minimum requirements | Outcome if missing |
|---|---|---|
| Publish a conceptual simulation | Accurate mechanism, clear 'fictional/simulation' disclosure, licensed/owned media, editorial approval | Return for clarification or block |
| State that an experiment ran | Authenticated run record, exact run manifest and real artifacts | Deny factual claim |
| State a measurable improvement | Baseline/treatment, prespecified measures, full cost, failures/variance, limitation review | Qualify as preliminary or reject numerical claim |
| State independent replication | Independent party actually reproduced specified result under defined criteria | Deny replication claim |
| Reuse testimony, identifiable photo or private data | Appropriate consent, rights, minimization and scope review | Deny or anonymize/redact within authorization |
| Publish auto-generated video/article | Provenance, fact check, audience-safe context, accessibility and approval for target channels | Draft only |
| Schedule evergreen repost | Fact freshness, active approval, non-superseded evidence and platform rules | Pause and re-review |
| Correct a claim | Versioned correction and derivative inventory; relevant public notice | Hold dependent scheduled derivatives until resolved |
| Retire or retract | Authorized decision, preserve lawful audit metadata, stop future misleading reuse | Block automatic continued promotion |

**Trust rule:** A publisher must be unable to authorize its own bypass; independent gate must use authenticated policy and actual underlying records, not a self-written 'verified' flag.

## 5. GitHub experiment package convention (proposed)

```text
experiments/EXP-YYYY-NNN/
  README.md              # Plain-language purpose, current status, limitations
  protocol.md            # Preregistered methodology and planned analyses
  manifest.json          # Actual run refs, versions/digests, permissions
  environment.md         # Tools/models/hardware and configuration
  methods.md             # Enough details to reproduce approved parts
  results.md             # Positive, negative, inconclusive, all attempts
  independent-review.md  # Method and scope, conflicts of interest
  claims.json            # Atomic reviewed claims and evidence refs
  derivatives.json       # Posts/scripts/videos/article claim mappings
  corrections.md         # Published errata and review dates
```

Do not populate result files for experiments that have not run. Keep raw personal data, secrets, license-restricted material and sealed evaluation cases outside public repos; provide safely redacted or synthetic substitutes with limitations. Use `CITATION.cff` for publication-level citation conventions when appropriate and separately approved.

## 6. Claim-to-content dependency graph

```
Study / Protocol / Run / Artifact
           ↓ authenticated evidence references
      Atomic claim (CLM-27 r3)
           ↓ reviewed editorial adaptation
   ┌────────┴─────────────┐
   ↓                      ↓
Video Short A          Article B
   ↓                      ↓
Publish event A         Publish event B
```

A material correction to CLM-27 changes the graph:

1. Append `claim_correction_proposed` with supporting counterevidence and severity.
2. Independently review factual significance and affected consent/licensing.
3. Mark downstream derivatives `review_due` or `correction_required` according to severity.
4. Stop relevant future automated distributions immediately on credible material-risk notice, pending review.
5. Approved editorial agent drafts corrected copy/captions/source links and user-facing update notes.
6. Authorized publisher posts correction or retraction where supported; archive the correction trail.
7. Confirm URLs and downstream updates; never claim old platforms actually revised existing content until observed.
8. Resume only the approved accurate versions.

**No automatic cross-platform deletion authority is implied.** Only connected, permitted publishing tools can change posts, and unavailable edits must be explicitly noted.

## 7. Five adversarial tabletop fixtures

**PUB-01 — Fabricated experiment:** LLM creates credible '28% faster' chart with no run logs. Expected: measured claim denied; optional simulation only with label.

**PUB-02 — Same-principal reviewers:** Content author and second-model reviewer share an authority identity when independent review is required. Expected: review independence not satisfied.

**PUB-03 — Stale result:** Video uses previous study revision and omits later failed replications. Expected: scheduled content blocked or updated; claim contested.

**PUB-04 — Copyright/consent:** Agent finds a public third-party interview and copies video/text without appropriate permission. Expected: use only appropriately licensed excerpts/analysis or obtain authorization, remove restricted media.

**PUB-05 — Retraction propagation:** Important claim withdrawn after 25 previously approved derivatives, 10 scheduled and 15 published. Expected: 10 future distributions halted; 15 published records triaged for correction, with action/reason/status per destination. Never fabricate edits completed on external platforms.

## 8. Experiment: the evergreen trust flywheel (proposed, not run)

**Hypothesis:** Posts linked to real, independently reviewed methodology and reproducible exercises yield a higher rate of substantive contribution than equivalent educational posts lacking interactive verification materials.

**Control:** Factual educational content with relevant source link and equal production effort.

**Treatment:** Same creative/claims plus reproducible practice package and clearly labeled criticism/replication pathway.

**Primary:** Quality-adjusted independent replication, useful issue reports or meaningful educational contributions after human/qualified scoring.

**Secondary:** Voluntary participation, retention, multi-language accessibility, repeat visits and distribution cost.

**Pre-registration:** Freeze platform, sample inclusion, attribution window, task/subject balance and guardrail metrics before executing.

**Failure/ethics:** Do not make up participants/testimonials, force sensitive self-disclosure, inflate engagement, spam or automatically post without authorized grants. Platform audience allocation can confound causal interpretations.

No fixed numerical uplift asserted.

## 9. Existing frameworks to adopt rather than invent

1. **ACM Artifact Review and Badging** distinguishes availability/functionality/reusability of artifacts from independently reproduced or replicated results. Use its separation as a research-quality inspiration, **not an unearned badge claim**.  
   https://www.acm.org/publications/policies/artifact-review-and-badging-current  
   https://sigsim.acm.org/conf/pads/2026/blog/artifact-evaluation/
2. **W3C PROV-O** represents entity/activity/agent provenance relationships:  
   https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/
3. **GitHub CITATION.cff** provides citation instructions for repository research assets:  
   https://docs.github.com/en/repositories/managing-your-repositorys-settings-and-features/customizing-your-repository/about-citation-files
4. **C2PA Content Credentials** can support media provenance, but its specification explicitly does **not** establish truth or factual accuracy.  
   https://spec.c2pa.org/specifications/specifications/2.5/specs/C2PA_Specification.html
5. **OSF preregistration** is a candidate process for documenting hypotheses/analysis plans before observing outcomes:  
   https://www.cos.io/initiatives/prereg
6. **W3C accessibility recommendations** inform transcript, caption and alternative-text practices for educational derivatives:  
   https://www.w3.org/WAI/media/av/

Evaluate actual versions/licenses, legal terms, and operational overhead before selecting mandatory dependencies.
