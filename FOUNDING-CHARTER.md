# Foromni Founding Charter

**Project:** Foromni  
**Organization:** Foromni-Network  
**Status:** Founding principles for community discussion; not a finalized legal constitution.

## Our North Star

Foromni is a global collaborative network connecting people, businesses, communities, developers, and intelligent systems so they can learn, contribute, build, and create shared value.

Our purpose is larger than any single technology. AI-native operations and open technology are important opportunities in the current era, but Foromni exists to support human collaboration and collective advancement as technologies evolve.

## Our original promise

The original Foromni project described a worldwide community where people could collaborate, explore, share insights, discover emerging projects, contribute to a public roadmap, and discuss the many dimensions of life in a professional, friendly, and kind-hearted environment.

Its original motto was:

> Build today, so others can enjoy tomorrow.

This charter preserves that founding spirit while setting out a direction for the next generation of the network.

## Mission

Make collaboration compound: help people and organizations contribute knowledge, capabilities, tools, and opportunities that others can build upon.

## Vision

A world in which participation is open, useful contributions are discoverable and reusable, independent communities can cooperate, and intelligent systems help people coordinate meaningful work without displacing human agency.

## Founding principles

1. **Open contribution.** People should have practical ways to participate, propose improvements, share knowledge, and build.
2. **Compounding knowledge.** Prefer reusable documentation, interoperable tools, transparent decisions, and contributions that create value beyond a single interaction.
3. **Human agency.** Technology and intelligent systems should expand people's ability to act, learn, create, and collaborate.
4. **Independent participation.** Members, businesses, projects, and communities retain their own identities, work, and decision-making rights.
5. **Interoperability.** Build toward open interfaces and clear contribution contracts so independent systems can cooperate.
6. **Trust and stewardship.** Protect privacy, security, attribution, and the integrity of shared infrastructure.
7. **Kind-hearted professionalism.** Debate ideas constructively; treat people with dignity across cultures and backgrounds.
8. **Build for tomorrow.** Make today's work understandable, maintainable, and useful to future participants.

## What Foromni is—and is not

Foromni is a collaborative network and an evolving ecosystem. It is not limited to cryptocurrency, a single product, a single company, or a single AI platform.

AI-native operations are a strategic initial focus: people and organizations should be able to discover, coordinate, and improve operations involving humans, software, and intelligent agents. This focus does not narrow the wider mission.

Foromni does not promise investment returns, token appreciation, or financial outcomes. Nothing in this repository is financial advice.

## Relationship with Internet Embassy

Internet Embassy is an independent founding ecosystem and contributor to the broader Foromni vision. It may build products, services, infrastructure, and operating practices that participate in Foromni.

Foromni should remain open to other independent businesses, communities, developers, and networks. Participation does not require becoming an Internet Embassy customer or subsidiary.

## Participation and governance

The network should evolve through transparent proposals, documented decisions, reviewable contributions, and proportionate responsibility.

Early maintainers steward the infrastructure while the community model is developed. No token, airdrop, voting, inflation, treasury, or financial mechanism is established by this charter. Any future mechanism requires separate research, explicit governance, legal and security review, and community discussion before implementation.

## Initial priorities

1. Establish a trustworthy, accessible public home for the network.
2. Publish the mission, contribution process, roadmap, and governance principles.
3. Build a useful network MVP around identity, discovery, collaboration, and reusable contributions.
4. Support AI-native operations as an interoperable capability rather than a closed vendor ecosystem.
5. Invite independent projects and communities to participate.
6. Measure usefulness, trust, participation, and durable contribution—not activity for its own sake.

## Historical note

The source for the original mission language in this first charter is the founder-provided transcription of the historical Foromni website, including its description of a worldwide Web3 community, collaborative forums, project exploration, open feature requests, and the motto “Build today, so others can enjoy tomorrow.” This is a preservation of that supplied historical text, not a claim that the original WordPress source code has been recovered.
