# Foromni Ecosystem Discovery Register

**Purpose:** Maintain a deliberate, evidence-based inventory of projects and communities that may benefit from reciprocal collaboration.

This is a research register, not an endorsement list, partner directory, or claim of affiliation. A project is not a Foromni partner unless the relationship is explicitly confirmed by both sides.

## Selection principles

Prioritize projects that:

- Solve a real problem relevant to collaboration, interoperability, AI-native operations, developer infrastructure, identity, security, or community coordination.
- Have a clear public purpose and a legitimate path for community contribution.
- Demonstrate healthy governance, maintainability, documentation, and security practices.
- Offer a specific opportunity to contribute or integrate without duplicating their core work.
- Have a plausible mutual benefit and respect independent ownership.

Avoid ranking projects solely by stars, follower counts, hype, or fundraising. Do not treat open issues as invitations to perform unsolicited work; follow each project's contribution process.

## Discovery workflow

1. Search for projects by capability or ecosystem gap.
2. Identify canonical websites, repositories, licenses, governance, and contribution guidance.
3. Review recent maintenance and security posture.
4. Complete a [Project Relationship Brief](templates/project-relationship-brief.md).
5. Identify one bounded contribution or integration opportunity.
6. Confirm the project's preferred engagement channel.
7. Reach out only when there is a specific, respectful, useful proposal.
8. Record responses and agreed next steps.
9. Deliver the contribution and document the outcome.
10. Invite reciprocal participation only when there is genuine mutual interest.

## Research quality requirements

Every entry must distinguish:

- **Verified fact:** directly supported by a current public source.
- **Inference:** a reasoned interpretation, clearly labeled.
- **Unknown:** information not yet established.

Recheck stale entries before outreach. Record the date reviewed. Never claim a project uses a license, accepts contributions, supports an integration, or endorses Foromni without evidence.

## Initial research themes

These are categories to investigate, not recommendations or claims about specific projects:

- Agent interoperability and tool protocols.
- Workflow orchestration and automation.
- Identity, authorization, and verifiable credentials.
- Open-source project discovery and contribution infrastructure.
- Knowledge management and portable provenance.
- Security, observability, and agent evaluation.
- Community governance and federated collaboration.

## Success measures

Track accepted upstream contributions, useful integrations, repeat collaborations, documented mutual benefits, and maintenance health. Do not optimize for outreach volume or superficial partner counts.
