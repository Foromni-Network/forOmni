# Foromni

**Build today, so others can enjoy tomorrow.**

The global collaborative network connecting people, businesses, communities and intelligent systems to build a better-connected world.

Foromni exists to make collaboration compound: helping people and organizations discover one another, share knowledge and capabilities, coordinate meaningful work, and create reusable value together.

Our mission is larger than any single technology. We are exploring open technology, collective intelligence, and AI-native operations as tools for human collaboration and advancement.

## What we're building

1. A network where people, businesses, projects, and capabilities can be discovered.
2. A contribution system that makes knowledge and useful work attributable and reusable.
3. Collaboration tools that help independent participants coordinate while retaining their identity and control.
4. Interoperability for human-led and AI-native operations across independent systems.

## How we grow

We do not intend to rebuild everything or extract value from other communities. We discover promising work, understand its context, contribute useful improvements upstream, integrate existing capabilities where appropriate, and invite reciprocal participation where there is mutual interest.

Internet Embassy is an independent founding ecosystem and early proving ground. Other organizations and communities should be able to participate on equal terms.

## Participate

We welcome contributions in research, product design, documentation, engineering, interoperability, and community stewardship. Please read the contribution guide and roadmap before proposing substantial changes.

Foromni is not limited to crypto, AI, or any one platform. Technology is a means; meaningful collaboration and shared progress are the purpose.
